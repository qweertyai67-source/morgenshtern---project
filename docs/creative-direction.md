# Creative direction

## Core idea

The experience is built around the tension between **MORGENSHTERN as a maximal public persona** and **Alisher as a more direct, vulnerable performer**. It deliberately avoids copying the current official tour storefront. Instead it combines a black/red high-impact shell with mineral tones, quieter editorial passages, and acoustic/live material.

## Research signals

- The official site uses stark black, white, and red; oversized uppercase typography; tour-first navigation; and direct links to Telegram, Instagram, YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Music: https://morgenshtern.love/
- Spotify's artist biography frames the recent chapter around recovery, spiritual searching, and the rock-leaning `ALISHER` EP: https://open.spotify.com/artist/2lWlPk7L8WEuYAsBGDTAnE
- Apple Music lists `Цветок` and `Dead Sea Live (Acoustic Live)` as 2026 releases: https://music.apple.com/us/artist/morgenshtern/1371400021
- The official YouTube channel published `Live from the Dead Sea`, recorded in Ein Bokek with a live band: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cdvu4rNEOqc
- The official SoundCloud account also places `Цветок` and the acoustic live tracks in the 2026 release sequence: https://soundcloud.com/morgenshtern-official
- Tour dates shown in the prototype were checked against the official tour site on 13 October 2026 and link back to that source rather than duplicating ticket-provider logic.

## Visitor journey

1. Immediate atmosphere and positioning.
2. Current live/release chapter.
3. Music discovery through official embeds.
4. A concise creative timeline.
5. Current tour dates and official ticket path.
6. City-demand and newsletter conversion points.

## Editorial guardrails

- Do not present the concept as an official artist resource until ownership is confirmed.
- Do not invent quotes, achievements, tour dates, prices, or personal claims.
- Keep changing facts in Sanity and verify them against primary sources before publication.
- Use official embeds and external links rather than redistributing music or video files.
